Desktop Software, Printed On Pink Paper

What This Is

A design system extracted from typesafe.ai and rebuilt for Instagram guides, carousels and reel cards. It looks like an early-80s windowed operating system (think Lisa or Mac System 1), laid over editorial Swiss typography, on saturated flat color.

The Feel

Precise, nerdy, warm. Big confident grotesk headlines up top. Tiny machine details around the edges: crop marks, base-64 noise, glyph corners, Game of Life pixels. Everything is on purpose, and nothing is rounded.

Everything Is A Window

Data, lists, tools, photos all live inside 1px windows with an ink title bar.

Color Is A Place

Each section is one flat world color. Sections change with hard cuts and no gradients.

Two Voices

The human voice is the grotesk. The machine voice is pixel and mono type. Don't mix them in one line.

05 / Texture

Grain, Dither, Dots

Dot Grid1px dots · 4px pitch · ink 60%

The "paper" inside every desktop frame. It works like a printed halftone field. .dots · a denser field (3px pitch) marks demo areas with .dots--fine. On Instagram use 2px dots on an 8px pitch.

Dither BlobsBayer 8×8 + jitter · pixelated · multiply

Big soft circles rendered in 1-bit ordered dither. The core is dense and the edge breaks into scattered single pixels. The site uses 2048² PNGs with mix-blend-mode: multiply. Here they're generated live with data-ros="dither".

Game Of Life2–3px cells · 8–10 fps

Living pixel dust drifting across the desktop. Gliders crawl out of the dither blobs. It stays subtle and sits behind the windows.

Film GrainfeTurbulence · multiply · 16%

This is the Team-page cloud photography: hot-pink clouds on a pale sky, heavily grained. Add .grain to photos and gradient fields. .grain--animated gives it a 4-step boil for reels.

Duotone CollagePatent drawings + punch cards, mapped to world colors
Company News

Blog art is a collage of public-domain patent figures ("FIG. 63", reference numerals, gears, hatching) and scanned IBM punch cards, cut into hard rectangles. Each panel gets one flat world color: grayscale image → contrast(1.35) → mix-blend-mode: multiply onto teal / sage / pink, or inverted to light lines on ink. Add a magenta category chip at the bottom-left. Good sources: Google Patents / USPTO drawings, Wikimedia punch-card scans.

07 / Motion
Linear, Stepped, Honest

    

      
Principle 01 · No Springs
Everything is linear or steps(). Nothing bounces, overshoots or eases in or out. It should feel like a 1983 computer drawing each frame.

      
Principle 02 · Hard Cuts
World colors switch instantly, and windows appear with no fade. Hover states invert in 0ms. If something "moves", it's a process running inside a window.

      
Principle 03 · Always Alive
Keep a few slow loops running at all times: the clock ticking, Game of Life cells crawling, sliders racing, the cube turning. The desktop is on.

Retro OS

      
For Reels
Animate at 12 fps "on twos" inside a 30 fps export, so motion reads as stepped. Open with the boot loader (≈1.2s), cut hard to the pink desktop, then let windows pop in one per beat at 0ms each. Hold the final frame for 1.5s so the text can be read.

09 / Rules
Do This, Not That

    

      
Do
    
        
  • ✣One flat world color per card or section.
    • 
        
  • ✣1px lines everywhere (2px on a 1080 canvas).
    • 
        
  • ✣Title Case headlines, tight tracking, 0.8 line-height.
    • 
        
  • ✣Frame every headline with crop marks.
    • 
        
  • ✣Put data and lists inside windows with ink title bars.
    • 
        
  • ✣Give every text block a micro label and a left rule.
    • 
        
  • ✣Use oddly precise numbers: 193.6x, $0.042.
    • 
        
  • ✣Hide real messages in base-64 blocks.
    • 
        
  • ✣Animate linearly or in steps. Keep a slow loop alive.
    • 
      

      
Don't
    
        
  • ✕No rounded corners, pills or soft shadows. Shadows are hard 2px offsets.
    • 
        
  • ✕No gradients on backgrounds. Grainy photos are the only exception.
    • 
        
  • ✕No pure #000 or #FFF surfaces.
    • 
        
  • ✕Don't set body copy in pixel type, or window chrome in the grotesk.
    • 
        
  • ✕Don't blend two world colors in one section.
    • 
        
  • ✕No springy or eased motion, and no fades between worlds.
    • 
        
  • ✕No emoji. Use ✣ ∵ ⩆ ♟ ▶▶▶ and box-drawing glyphs.
    • 
        
  • ✕Don't put paper text on pink below 40px.
    • 
        
  • ✕Don't copy the source's logo, cube mark or copy. Borrow the language only.
